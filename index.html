Welcome to the Toybox!

This website is a small passion project of mine. I wanted to review HTML and CSS, learn more, and make something fun for myself that others can enjoy. I actually got the inspiration to make this website from a youtube video talking about the indie web and how it brings back the nostalgia of the old internet before everything changed. Growing up around an older brother who played gamecube and PS2 games, I grew fond of what I would call "retro media." I wanted to be part of that creative and nostalgic side of things. Whether it's sucking up a ghost in Luigi's Mansion, or wacking someone with a Keyblade in Kingdom Hearts -- these are the memories that will stick with me for the rest of my life. I want to preserve my passion for old games, shows, and ugly red and yellow websites that make your eyes bleed.
